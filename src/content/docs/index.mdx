## What OFM does

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="One model, many firewalls" icon="puzzle">
		OFM keeps its own standard configuration model. Adapters translate it
		for each supported operating system, starting with OPNsense.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Versioned configuration" icon="document">
		Every change is a commit. Review, deploy and roll back with a clear
		history. Local changes on a firewall are detected and can be imported.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Live log viewer" icon="magnifier">
		Follow traffic as it happens. Build filters by drag and drop and spot
		actions at a glance through colour-coded rows.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Metrics over time" icon="information">
		System, interfaces, services, routing and VPN in small, comparable
		graphs, with OFM commits and deployments marked on the timeline.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Built for many appliances" icon="list-format">
		Manage firewalls in bulk. Zones and aliases resolve per appliance, and
		policy templates and sub-policies make rules reusable.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Open by design" icon="open-book">
		Every component is open source and free of cost. AGPL-3.0 for the
		project, BSD-2-Clause for the firewall connector.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
